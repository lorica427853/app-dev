# About the movie
Tangled is an animated Disney movie about Rapunzel, a young princess with magical long blonde hair. She has spent most of her life inside a tower until she meets a man named Flynn Rider. Together, they go on an adventure that helps Rapunzel discover the truth about her past and find her real family.
